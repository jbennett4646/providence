The Seven Virtues

I Prudence Logic, and stating the other side’s case before answering it.
II Justice History read from sources, so judgment rests on what people did.
III Fortitude Hard subjects kept hard. Latin is not made easier to be kind.
IV Temperance A daily rhythm, finished work, and a school day that ends.
V Faith Sacred history read whole, in its own words.
VI Hope Psalms, poems and speeches carried into adult life.
VII Charity Recitation to a listener, because clear speech is for another person.
Character is formed on purpose or by accident.
